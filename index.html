Battle Mechanics

🎴 Team Formation

  • A team consists of 5 cards.
  • Only 1 card is active on the field at a time.
  • The remaining 4 cards stay on the bench.
  • You choose the placement order from first to last.
  • When the active card is defeated, the next card in the bench order replaces it.

🌐 Global Turn

Based on current observations, a Global Turn is an overall battle cycle involving both fielded cards completing their turns, including applicable off-field ability effects from benched cards.

Status: Observed / needs further testing.

🪑 Off-Field Effects

An Off-Field Effect is an effect within a card's ability that can still work while the card remains on the bench.

The ability itself is not necessarily entirely "Off-Field"; some of its effects may require the card to be on the field.

About This Database

This is a fan-made, community-maintained database for the Roblox game Anime Card: Anomaly.

The goal is to document cards, rarities, stats, odds, abilities, and battle mechanics as accurately as possible.

Card count numbers such as #177/263 are intentionally not stored as card data.
