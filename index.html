Battle Mechanics

🎴 Team Formation

  • A team consists of 5 cards.
  • Only 1 card is active on the field at a time.
  • The remaining 4 cards stay on the bench.
  • You choose the placement order from first to last.
  • When the active card is defeated, the next card in the bench order replaces it.

🌐 Global Turn

Based on current observations, a Global Turn refers to an overall battle cycle involving both fielded cards completing their actions, including applicable off-field effects.

Status: Observed / needs further testing.

🪑 Field & Bench

Field: The currently active card.

Bench: The four waiting cards. Some abilities can affect battle while the card remains off-field.

⚠️ Accuracy Policy

Mechanics are marked as confirmed, observed, or unconfirmed as more information becomes available.

About This Database

This is a fan-made, community-maintained database for the Roblox game Anime Card: Anomaly.

The goal is to document cards, abilities, rarities, battle mechanics, and eventually team synergies.

Version 1 currently contains sample entries that can be expanded by editing cards.json.
